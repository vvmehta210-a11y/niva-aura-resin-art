Welcome to Niva Aura

At Niva Aura Resin Art Studio, we specialize in transforming high-potency liquid epoxy resin, real minerals, 24K gold foil, and preserved botanicals into breathtaking bespoke art pieces. Each creation is poured, cured, polished, and finished by hand with precision.

Whether it is an ocean-wave wall clock echoing the rhythm of sea tides, a crystal-clear coaster set enclosing hand-pressed lotus blooms, or an executive desk nameplate cast in deep sapphire resin, our passion lies in crafting timeless elegance for your living spaces and corporate environments.

💎 Premium Materials

We use non-yellowing, food-safe, UV-resistant epoxy resin engineered for durability and glass-like mirror clarity.

🎨 100% Handcrafted

Every piece is unique and one-of-a-kind. No two resin pours are ever identical, guaranteeing exclusive art for your home.

✨ Custom Orders

From custom color palettes and personalized initial keychains to vanity wash basins, we bring your vision to life.
